Pakistan Volleyball Team Remains Unbeaten in CAVA Championship

Published: July 30, 2026

Pakistan's men's volleyball team completed the round-robin stage of the CAVA Men's Volleyball Championship 2026 without losing a match.

The national team won all five of its round-robin matches and remained unbeaten throughout the stage of the tournament.

Pakistan Complete Perfect Round-Robin Stage

Pakistan finished the round-robin stage with five victories and won all fifteen sets played during the competition.

The strong performance helped Pakistan finish at the top of the round-robin standings and secure a place in the knockout stage.

Strong Performance Against Kazakhstan

Pakistan ended the round-robin stage with a straight-set win against Kazakhstan.

The victory continued Pakistan's unbeaten run and showed the team's strong form during the championship.

What Happens Next

After completing the round-robin stage, Pakistan moved forward to the knockout rounds of the CAVA Men's Volleyball Championship.

The team will aim to continue its winning performance and compete for the championship title.

Conclusion

Pakistan's unbeaten performance has been an important achievement in the CAVA Men's Volleyball Championship 2026.
