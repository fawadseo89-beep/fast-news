South Korea Hockey Team Arrives for Four-Match Pakistan Series

The South Korean national hockey team has arrived in Islamabad to take part in a four-match international Test series against Pakistan. The tour is expected to provide valuable international competition for both teams.

The matches will give Pakistan's players an opportunity to test their skills against an experienced international side. Hockey supporters will be watching the series closely as Pakistan continues its efforts to improve its international performance.
